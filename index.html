About us

Empowering visions, delivering results.

FAHHM Engineering Services (SMC) Private Limited is a design, consultancy and construction firm integrating visionary design, stringent testing protocols, meticulous project management and end-to-end construction. Led by Engr. Junaid Ahmad, we take a project from architectural concept through engineering and testing to a built, delivered structure — under one window.

Our vision is to be globally recognised as pioneers in construction consultancy — synonymous with a standard of excellence that incorporates integrity and a comprehensive approach, transforming architectural blueprints into enduring structures of profound significance. Our values: commitment, client satisfaction, honesty, clarity and a task-oriented approach.

FAQ

The questions we get asked.

Do you handle design and construction both?
Yes — we're a single-window firm covering architecture and engineering design, consultancy and testing, and construction. You get one accountable team from concept through to a finished structure.
What services do you offer?
Architecture, structural, interior, urban / town planning, MEP, infrastructure and GIS design, plus project management, environmental studies, topographic survey, testing and construction — a complete one-window solution from design to delivery.
Why consult FAHHM?
Trusted partners, high quality standards, an empathetic approach, aggressive timelines, systematic planning and a single-window solution — that's what we bring to every engagement.
Where are you located?
Based in F-11 Markaz, Islamabad — we work with clients across Pakistan.
Do you handle testing and approvals?
Yes — stringent testing and QA protocols are built into our process, and we prepare code-compliant, tender-ready documentation and approvals.
How do we get started?
Send your project details via the form or call us — we'll set up a consultation and map out scope, timeline and next steps.
